# IRPA Governance-to-Management and Signature Authority Matrix

**Status:** Draft for Board review. This document does not appoint a signatory, create a delegation, amend the Constitution, alter a bank mandate, or activate application permissions.

## 1. Governing principle

**The Board governs. The Executive Director manages. Departments execute within documented delegations. Job descriptions prescribe authority and limitations.**

The authority chain is:

1. **Board of Directors** — collective governance, strategic oversight and matters reserved by the Constitution, law, policy or Board resolution.
2. **Executive Director** — overall management authority, accountable and reporting to the Board.
3. **Directorate / Department Head** — manages the assigned portfolio under written delegation from the Executive Director.
4. **Unit Head / Supervisor** — exercises only authority expressly sub-delegated in writing.
5. **Officer / Specialist / Assistant** — performs assigned preparation, verification, recommendation or execution duties; no implied approval or signature authority.

The hierarchy is a reporting and delegation model, not a claim that every position is currently staffed.

## 2. Governance and management are separate domains

| Domain | Authority | Permitted scope | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance | Board of Directors acting collectively | Strategy, oversight, reserved approvals, appointment/accountability of the Executive Director, approval of policies and delegations | Individual Board offices do not independently exercise collective Board powers unless expressly delegated |
| Management | Executive Director | Overall organisational operations, implementation of Board decisions, coordination of departments, management approvals within approved policy/budget/delegation | Cannot override Board-reserved matters, law, Constitution, bank mandates or mandatory controls |
| Departmental management | Department / Directorate Head | Department plans, staff supervision, operational decisions and approvals expressly delegated | Cannot exceed, vary or sub-delegate beyond the written ED delegation |
| Unit supervision | Unit Head / Supervisor, where established | Day-to-day supervision and specified operational decisions | Only the written sub-delegation; no authority inferred from title or seniority |
| Staff execution | Officers and other staff | Prepare, verify, recommend, implement and maintain records as assigned | No implied approval, final authorisation, signature or release authority |

## 3. Departmental hierarchy and reporting

All management directorates and departments report administratively through the Executive Director. The Executive Director reports to the Board.

Each department must identify, in its approved structure:
- the department head and the position to which that head reports;
- subordinate units and their heads, where established;
- each position's supervisor and direct reports;
- vacant positions and approved acting arrangements;
- the decisions delegated to each level; and
- escalation routes to the Executive Director.

The system's hierarchy template is:

**Executive Director → Directorate / Department Head → Unit Head / Supervisor (if established) → Officer / Specialist → Assistant / Field or Support Staff.**

Special reporting protections:
- **Board Secretariat:** reports to the Board for governance business.
- **Executive Office:** supports and reports to the Executive Director.
- **Internal Audit and Assurance:** administrative coordination through the Executive Director, with functional independence and direct access/reporting to the Board; it must not audit its own work.
- **Research, Statistics and Knowledge:** administrative accountability to the Executive Director; research integrity, ethics and clearance decisions remain protected by approved research policy.

## 4. Signature and decision matrix

| Instrument / decision | Prepare / initiate | Review / verify | Recommend | Approve / authorise | Sign / execute | Release / publish |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board resolutions and governance instruments | Board Secretariat / authorised officer | Governance review or committee | Relevant committee / ED where appropriate | Board collectively where reserved | Only office-holder expressly authorised by Constitution, resolution or legal instrument | Board Secretariat after certification |
| Strategy, annual plans, budgets and management policies | Relevant department / Executive Office | Finance and technical departments | Executive Director | Board where reserved; otherwise ED within approved delegation | ED or specifically delegated officer | Executive Office / responsible department |
| Routine departmental operations and programme delivery | Assigned officer / unit | Unit supervisor / department head | Department head | Department head within written ED delegation | Department head or named delegate within scope | Responsible department |
| Human resources and personnel decisions | HR / responsible department | HR and relevant supervisor | Department head and HR | ED within approved HR policy; Board for reserved appointments | ED or specifically delegated HR signatory | HR after approval |
| Finance, expenditure, payments and bank instruments | Finance officer / budget holder | Independent finance review | Budget holder / Finance lead | Approver under approved financial delegation | Current bank-mandated signatories for bank instruments; otherwise specifically authorised signatory | Separate payment releaser / Treasury |
| Procurement and contracts | Requesting department / Procurement | Procurement, technical and Finance review | Evaluation committee / Procurement lead | Authority under approved procurement policy and delegation | Named contract signatory under written delegation and law | Procurement / contract custodian |
| Research ethics, restricted data and research release | Research team / data custodian | Research governance, ethics and data-protection review | Research Director / designated lead | Named authority under approved research policy | Research Director or expressly delegated authority where required | Named custodian after clearance |
| Communications, website and social media | Communications author | Independent editorial / technical reviewer | Communications lead | Approver under written Communications delegation; ED retains management oversight | Normally not applicable; approval identity and content version recorded | Authorised publisher after approval |
| Asset, records and data disposal / transfer | Custodian / responsible department | Finance, Records, IT or Compliance as relevant | Department head | ED within delegation; Board where reserved/material | Named authorised officer | Custodian after approval and register update |

### Instrument-specific safeguards

- **Board instruments:** a Board resolution or other valid legal instrument must identify the power, scope, authorised office-holder, duration and any conditions. A Board title alone is not enough.
- **Banking and payments:** the current bank mandate governs bank execution. This matrix assigns no bank signatories or monetary limits. Payment preparation, approval and release must remain appropriately separated.
- **Procurement and contracts:** the approved procurement policy and written delegation determine thresholds and signatories. Do not infer a threshold or contract authority from job grade.
- **Research:** restricted data remains denied by default. A role name, publication status or generic Administrator access does not constitute research clearance.
- **Communications:** author and approver must be distinct unless a narrowly scoped, formally approved exception applies. Publication remains subject to the system's publishing controls.
- **HR:** no person may approve their own appointment, remuneration, expenses or disciplinary outcome.

## 5. Mandatory job-description authority fields

Every approved job description must specify:

1. Position ID, approved title, department and unit.
2. Purpose, duties and decision-making scope.
3. Reports-to relationship and positions supervised.
4. Actions permitted: initiate, review, verify, recommend, approve, authorise, sign and release.
5. Financial/procurement limits, currency, period and source of approval, if any.
6. Decisions expressly prohibited or reserved to a higher authority.
7. Escalation triggers and the approving authority.
8. Required reports, records, evidence and audit responsibilities.
9. Delegation identifier, version, effective date, review/expiry date and revocation status.
10. Acting arrangements, where applicable, including written scope and dates.

A job description defines the position's limits; a separate current appointment establishes who occupies the position; a written delegation establishes the power actually conferred. All three must be present where applicable.

## 6. Universal authorisation rules

1. Default decision is **DENY** unless the current appointment, job description, delegation, workflow scope and server-side controls all authorise the action.
2. Authority follows the approved reporting line and written delegation, not seniority, department membership or a job title alone.
3. The Board acts collectively unless a valid instrument expressly delegates a defined power.
4. The Executive Director is the overall management authority and reports to the Board.
5. Department heads exercise only authority expressly delegated by the Executive Director.
6. Sub-delegation is prohibited unless the original written delegation permits it.
7. A vacant or unconfirmed position carries no authority.
8. Acting appointments must be written, scoped and time-bound.
9. A person holding multiple positions must select the capacity relevant to the action. Permissions do not combine automatically.
10. No self-approval. Preparation, review, approval, signature and release must be separated where required by policy and risk.
11. Every approval/signature records actor identity, capacity, appointment, delegation reference/version, action, applicable limit, timestamp, document version/hash and reviewer/approver evidence.
12. The matrix does not replace the Constitution, applicable law, bank mandate, procurement policy, approved HR/finance policies or Board resolutions.

## 7. Evidence required before activation

For each permission, the system must be able to retrieve:
- approved organisational structure and department hierarchy;
- current appointment or acting appointment;
- approved job description;
- Board resolution or Executive Director delegation, as applicable;
- instrument-specific policy, budget or clearance;
- workflow approval evidence; and
- immutable audit evidence of the action and document version.

If any required evidence is missing, expired, revoked, ambiguous or inconsistent, the action must fail closed and be escalated.

## 8. Explicitly unassigned matters

This design intentionally does **not** invent:
- legal signatories for Board or contract instruments;
- bank signatories or payment limits;
- procurement value thresholds;
- names of department heads or unit heads;
- individual staff appointments or acting appointments;
- powers not already granted by a valid Board resolution or written ED delegation.

These require formal organisational approval and supporting records before runtime activation.

## 9. Implementation boundary

This is the policy and data-model foundation. It does not by itself prove that every portal, Firebase rule, Cloudflare Gateway route or production workflow enforces the matrix. Runtime enforcement requires a separate cross-portal permission crosswalk, server-side checks, negative/positive tests, staging acceptance and explicit approval before any production rules change or deployment.
